# Nino D — Pre-Launch Plan

**Phase:** Pre-launch (now → late September)
**#1 Goal this phase:** Build the waitlist for Edition 01.
**Commercial north star:** Sell the scarf editions (100 per edition, €450, locked forever).
**At launch the #1 goal flips** from *Join the list* to *Claim Edition 01*.

Everything below exists to turn a cold visitor into a subscriber who is primed to buy when editions open.

---

## Global / system-wide

1. **One CTA hierarchy.** Each page has exactly one *primary* action and the rest are visibly secondary. No more two equal-weight buttons side by side (e.g. the homepage's twin "Explore" buttons). Primary = filled (ink-black → oxblood). Secondary = outline or quiet text link.

2. **Primary action everywhere = Join the waitlist.** A single, well-designed Join block (strong copy, not a bare email field) becomes the closing moment of every page. Offer is tied to scarcity: *first access to 100 numbered pieces before they open publicly.*

3. **Site-wide launch signal.** A slim announcement bar in the header: *"Edition 01 — opening late September. Join for first access."* Reinforces the one goal and creates anticipation/time pressure on every page.

4. **Verb shift.** Replace soft lateral verbs (Explore, Inquire) as *primary* actions with the Join offer now. Reserve **Claim / Secure** for launch.

5. **Navigation, pre-launch.** Keep it lean: Visual Archive · Wearable Archive · About. Hide **The Vault** (nothing to buy yet). Keep **Inquire** available but de-emphasized (paintings, press, collaborations).

---

## Page by page

### Homepage — from "summary" to "trailer with one door"
- Hero: one emotional line + **one** primary CTA → *Join the waitlist.* A quiet secondary link → *Enter Nino's world (About).*
- Compress the repeating sections into a short desire-building sequence that ends on the Join block.
- Replace the bottom "Acquisition / contact form" section with the Join block + the September/scarcity message.
- Result: every scroll path ends at *"join before it opens."*

### About — the emotional engine (keep, fix only the ending)
- The page itself stays as is — it's the strongest asset.
- **Highest-leverage single change:** its current ending ("Acquire a work. Begin an inquiry.") dead-ends. Replace with: *"You've seen the world. Wear a fragment of it — Edition 01 opens in September. Join for first access."* Primary CTA = Join.

### Visual Archive — desire-builder that feeds the waitlist
- Keep the depth; it's good. But point its energy forward, not sideways.
- Where a painting becomes a scarf, say so: *"This work becomes Edition 0X — join to acquire it in September."*
- Strengthen the existing "From Visual to Wearable Archive" bridge; end it on Join.
- Keep "inquire about the painting" available but secondary.

### Wearable Archive — pre-launch "preview" mode
- Replace the live **Claim** button with: *"Edition 0X — Opening September"* + **Join for first access** (primary).
- Keep the strong elements: source-painting ↔ silk pairing, €450, edition of 100, "opens once, closes forever."
- This becomes the peak desire + scarcity moment — all funneling to Join.
- The Claim → Vault → Secure flow we built stays dormant, switched on at launch.

### The Vault / checkout — built, dormant
- Already built. Hidden from nav pre-launch. Goes live at launch.

### Inquire / Acquire — keep, de-emphasize
- Stays for paintings, collaborations, press. Not a primary path.

### Footer
- Upgrade "Join the Archive" capture to match the new primary offer (consistent copy).
- Real Instagram / Pinterest links (you to provide).

## The launch flip (late September) — one coordinated change

- Header bar: *"Edition 01 — open now — X of 100 remain."*
- Wearable Archive: *"Opening September"* → live **Claim Edition No. X.**
- Site-wide primary CTA: Join → **Claim.**
- The Vault returns to the nav.
- Email the waitlist first: a short early-access window (e.g. 48h) before public opening.
